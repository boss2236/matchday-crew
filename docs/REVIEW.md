# Independent review

A second model, from a different family than the writer, re-checked lines the crew airs against the exact evidence pack each writer was given. The crew's own fact checker is code (`src/agents/evidence.ts`): it checks every number, name and cited event, and rejects lines before they air. This review asks a model whether anything a line *says* goes beyond the evidence, which code cannot judge (an outcome implied, a claim in words rather than digits), and how clear the line is for its audience.

Generated by `scripts/review.ts` on 2026-10-22. Reviewer: **gpt-6.1-sol** (GitHub Copilot, through the Copilot SDK). Lines: 150 of 819 aired lines, an even spread over the 4 published fixtures and 6 languages (English, Arabic, Spanish, French, Hindi, Japanese), replayed from the response cache. Narrator lines are also checked against the Tactician insight they were given, which itself passed the same checks.

> Note on this run: Recap lines are listed as English below, but each was written in its audience's language (the script labelled them wrongly; fixed since). After this review, the yards it flagged became a fact-checker rule, and the affected lines were rewritten.
